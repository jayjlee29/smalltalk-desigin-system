Small status label. Used for board 활성/비활성 status, account-type chips (ISA/연금저축/IRP/일반), and article hashtags.

```jsx
<Badge tone="blue">활성</Badge>
<Badge tone="purple">연금저축</Badge>
```

`pill` (default true) gives fully-rounded pill shape (status/tags); set `false` for the small squared tag chip variant.
